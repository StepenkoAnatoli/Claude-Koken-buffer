## Context

Review of `en2`, recorded in `cost-cut-plan/re1/re1-review-of-copilot-v2.md`.

## What holds

**v2 is accepted as the stronger plan on governance.** Ordering converges with the earlier review: prefix
diet first, rate routing second (provider-gated), session shaping conditional, retrieval last.

## What must change — seven amendments

Five are contributions v2 lacks:

1. **Facts-sweep split** — a ≤1h sweep of facts that already exist, separate from the benchmark programme.
2. **Piggyback protocol** — measurement rides on real work rather than requiring dedicated runs.
3. **Candidate payload-tax rubric line** — score each candidate on what it adds to the resident payload.
4. **EN3a/EN3b split** — compact-before-idle is not the same intervention as session-length shaping.
5. **EN1 cache-freeze half** — the freeze rule is load-bearing and was folded invisibly into the diet.

## Withdrawn

One earlier position is withdrawn: **phase-1 bundling of prefix diet with rate routing.** v2's causality rule
is correct — bundling two changes destroys attribution.

## New dominant risk

**Governance overhead that never yields a verified saving for a one-person project.** The plan can cost more
than it saves in process alone.

## Naming

Canonical intervention IDs `I1`–`I4`, with v2's `EN1–EN4` and the earlier `L1–L7` recorded as aliases.

## Evidence status

External community measurements throughout, cited as motivation only.

## Requested of the recipient

Agreement on the amendments and on an explicit evidence boundary.
